# Progress-Pairwise Critic V1 Preregistration

## Scope

This study asks whether a deployment-visible action-aware critic can rank the
eight candidates generated at each frozen Progress-Pairwise boundary. It does
not reuse or modify the stopped 288-episode IQL critic and does not establish
online QGF efficacy.

## Frozen Evidence

- Population report SHA:
  `501d7ce97068100eec28aa1db070d70680411e14e223be9058919b2cc508f1b6`.
- Independent audit SHA:
  `d365e4a24d174eaeb329e0871464e7f32a0d60687f59a23dcb97aa11f234ae0d`.
- Dataset: 14 complete physical groups, four task families, 112 real branches.
- Unavailable groups 3 and 5 remain scientific denominators and provide no
  fabricated branch labels.

## Inputs And Targets

- Action-aware input: frozen boundary observation/state representation plus
  the candidate's first 16 normalized 14-D actions and candidate-kind flag.
- State-only control: identical boundary representation with no candidate
  action content; it receives the same target and training budget.
- Permutation control: action-aware architecture trained after independently
  permuting candidate actions within each physical group while keeping labels
  fixed. The permutation seed is tied to the training seed.
- No official success, simulator predicate, future execution length, final
  state, progress value, or branch outcome may enter any model input.
- Primary target: within-group pairwise preference induced by horizon-32
  continuous progress. Ties are excluded using an absolute difference of
  `1e-6` or less.
- Secondary target: horizon-16 preference, reported but not used to select a
  checkpoint or configuration.

## Splits And Seeds

- Physical groups are indivisible. All eight candidates from a group stay in
  the same fold.
- Use four group-disjoint folds generated by sorting
  `sha256(episode_id + "|pairwise-critic-v1")` within each family and assigning
  round-robin. No result-dependent reassignment is allowed.
- Training seeds: `202641601`, `202641602`, `202641603`.
- Every seed trains the action-aware, state-only, and within-group permutation
  models with identical optimizer, update count, width, depth, and early-stop
  policy. The update count must be frozen after a CPU shape/cost pilot and
  before the first scientific fit.

## Metrics And Gate

- Primary: physical-group-equal held-out pairwise accuracy at horizon 32.
- Secondary: per-family accuracy, Kendall tau within candidate banks,
  calibration of predicted preference margins, and horizon-16 accuracy.
- Uncertainty: 10,000 physical-group bootstrap draws, seed `202641701`.
- The action-aware model must, for every training seed, outperform both the
  state-only and permutation controls on pooled group-equal accuracy.
- The pooled bootstrap lower confidence bound for both action-aware minus
  state-only and action-aware minus permutation must be greater than zero.
- Action sensitivity must be positive: replacing an action with another
  candidate from the same bank must change the predicted score beyond the
  frozen numerical tolerance in at least 90% of non-tied comparisons.
- Any failed seed or control freezes `NO_STABLE_PROGRESS_PAIRWISE_CRITIC`.
  There is no seed addition, fold change, update extension, task removal, or
  threshold relaxation.

## Release

Passing this gate freezes the critic, normalizer, action support, and abstention
rule, and only then allows a separately preregistered held-out, same-compute
online comparison. Passing critic metrics is not itself a QGF benefit claim.

No GPU training is authorized by this document alone. Source, split manifest,
cost pilot, exact update count, and launch registration must be frozen first.
